Full-sequence position error (cm)

Experiment details

One trajectory per task, one replay per force level, without re-optimization. Forces oppose the operating hand or foot during the interaction window. Errors are full-sequence bilateral means. This tests resistance sensitivity, not object-contact success.

Experiment details

Same checkpoint, initial pose and sampling noise across conditions. The baseline uses recorded images rather than a live robot camera. One example per task; videos are qualitative, not task-success measurements.

Transfer & comparison scope

Zero-shot means transfer without target-task robot demonstrations, after task-specific human-data training. The 50-task study evaluates motion alignment; the HuMI-style comparison isolates initialization under the same controller.

Rendering preserves the recorded background and does not reconstruct the robot’s changing 3D viewpoint.
